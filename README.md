# Article 53, The Strict Rule Doesn't Add Storefront

Static explainer for the Article 53 ground-floor commercial debate at the
2026 Arlington Annual Town Meeting.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step required.

## Argument

Article 53 (Cullinane & Slotnick) and the two substitute motions on the
floor change how the 60% ground-floor commercial requirement in
§ 5.8.4.E(1) is measured. Three rules end up on the table:

| Rule                          | Denominator                              | Outcome on Docket #3862 stress-test lot |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Current ARB practice          | Enclosed ground floor (GFA only)         | Bonus floor built; storefront, homes, tax base |
| Macaluso substitute (5/6/26)  | GFA, but ≥ 50% Building Area             | Same outcome here; binds on smaller-podium lots |
| Cullinane-Slotnick (4/28/26)  | Full footprint, parking included         | Bonus likely skipped on podium-garage lots |

The page argues, with bylaw and code citations, that under the strict
rule the additional ~574 sf of commercial in the 126 Broadway model cannot readily come from
parking, bike room, elevator, lobby/egress, or loading/utilities. So the
bonus floor may not get built, and the town gets less of everything.

## Page structure

1. Hero, the headline argument.
2. Side-by-side scoreboard of the three rules.
3. "Find the 574 square feet", five locked-door buttons; each click
   reveals a bylaw or code citation explaining why that space cannot be
   converted to commercial.
4. Why Macaluso is also worse, explains the 50% Building Area floor.
5. Tax base benchmark, using two cleaner corridor mixed-use assessment comps.
6. Sources, full citations.

## Calculations and data sources

- The simulation uses withdrawn 126 Broadway / Docket #3862 because it is the stress-test podium-garage layout.
- 259 Broadway and the new 126-128 Broadway plan appear to pass under Cullinane; they are useful pass-case examples but do not show the tradeoff.
- 126 Broadway model: 5,407 sf parcel, ~3,401 sf ground-floor footprint, 1,817 sf enclosed ground-floor denominator, 1,467 sf proposed commercial, 5 ground-floor garage spaces, 14 homes (3 affordable).
- Cullinane requires 60% × 3,401 = 2,041 sf → +574 sf vs. ARB practice.
- Page tax benchmark: $319 / gross sf, the lower of the two recent new-construction corridor comps used on the page: 1157-1163 Mass Ave at $319 / gross sf and 1500 Mass Ave at $347 / gross sf.
- Arlington FY2026 tax rate: $10.67 per $1,000 of assessed value.
- Childcare sizing: 606 CMR 7.07, including 35 sf indoor activity space per child and 75 sf outdoor play area per child outside at one time.
